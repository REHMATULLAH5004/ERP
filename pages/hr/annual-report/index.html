Annual Attendance & Leave Report

"All Employees" shows one row per employee for the whole year. Pick a single employee to see a month-by-month breakdown instead. Required Hours = 9h on a normal working day, 5h on the half-day adjacent to a Sat/Sun off day, 0h on the employee's own off day or a public holiday -- the same rule used for overtime elsewhere in HR. A year still in progress only counts days up to today. No month before September 2026 is counted, and annual leave entitlement is prorated to match (e.g. a 24-day/year entitlement shows as 8 for 2026's Sep-Dec-only scope) -- same cutoff the Leave and Payroll pages use.

Pick a year (and optionally an employee) and click Generate.
